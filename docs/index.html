UNISTDisasterHousingBot

A research crawler operated for an academic study at UNIST (Ulsan National Institute of Science and Technology), Republic of Korea.

User-Agent

Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; UNISTDisasterHousingBot/0.1; +https://brad9704.github.io/temp-housing-cases/)

The bot always sends this exact string. It never uses browser or other crawler identities, never rotates User-Agents or IP proxies, and does not spoof TLS fingerprints.

What it does

The study documents problems that arise in disaster evacuation shelters, prolonged shelter living, and temporary housing: what went wrong, why, and how it developed. The bot collects:

  • news articles from a small number of outlets per country, limited to publicly accessible pages;
  • public reports from humanitarian organisations, through their published APIs where these exist.

Most requests read sitemaps or archive pages to find relevant articles, then fetch the articles themselves.

How it behaves

  • It follows robots.txt, including Crawl-delay. If robots.txt cannot be fetched because of a server error or a failed connection, it treats the whole site as disallowed.
  • It sends at most one request at a time to any domain, spaced by a random 2–5 second delay or the site's Crawl-delay, whichever is longer.
  • It honours Retry-After on 429/503 responses and backs off exponentially.
  • It stops collecting from a domain after repeated 403/429 responses or a bot challenge. It does not attempt to solve or get around challenges.
  • It never bypasses paywalls or logins.
  • It excludes sites that reserve text-and-data mining or AI use in their robots.txt or terms.

How the data is used

  • Collected text is used only for internal, non-commercial research analysis and is never redistributed.
  • Published outputs contain metadata, coded fields, and short quotations with citations only.
  • Content is not used to train AI models.

Blocking or limiting the bot

To block the bot entirely, add this to your robots.txt:

User-agent: UNISTDisasterHousingBot
Disallow: /

To slow it down, add Crawl-delay: <seconds> under the same User-agent line.

Contact

For questions, blocking requests, or problems caused by the bot, please open an issue on the project repository. The crawler code is public in the same repository.
